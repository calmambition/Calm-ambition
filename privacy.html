Policy

What I collect, why I collect it, who else handles it, and how you can see, correct or delete it.

Last updated 28 October 2026

Who I am

Calm Ambition is the coaching business of Priyanka Jain (ABN 17 581 647 628), a health and wellness coach based in Sydney, New South Wales. In this policy, "I", "me" and "Calm Ambition" mean Priyanka Jain trading as Calm Ambition.

Health and wellness coaching counts as a health service under Australian privacy law. That means I follow the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Health Privacy Principles in the Health Records and Information Privacy Act 2002 (NSW), even though Calm Ambition is a small business.

What I collect

When you browse this website

I use Google Analytics to see how people use the site: which pages you visit, how long you stay, your device and browser type, your approximate location, and whether you start or finish the burnout check-in. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser to do this. I see these figures as totals, and I don't use them to identify you. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

You can block analytics cookies in your browser settings or with Google's opt-out add-on. The site works the same without them.

When you fill in the discovery call form

Your first and last name, email address, what you do, and anything you write about what's bringing you here.

When you take the burnout check-in

Your answers to twelve questions about sleep, energy, tension, focus, motivation and relationships, and anything you type in the two free-text questions. This is health information, which the law treats as sensitive. I only collect it with your consent, and you give that consent when you start the check-in. If you ask for your readout by email, I collect your email address too. You can skip that and read your readout on screen. The check-in section below explains each step.

When you become a client

Your contact details; the health, wellbeing and work information you share on your intake form and in sessions; my session notes; your goals and progress; anything you record in the Calm Ambition companion app; and the billing details I need to invoice you. I don't store credit card numbers.

When you email me

Your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message.

Staying anonymous

You can browse this site without giving your name or email address. The burnout check-in offers to email you your readout, but you can skip that and read it on screen. It never asks for your name. You need to identify yourself if you ask me to contact you or become a client, because I can't coach someone I can't identify.

Why I use your information

  • To reply when you contact me and to run discovery calls
  • To generate your check-in readout and, if you ask, email it to you
  • To deliver coaching: preparing for sessions, tracking your progress, and supporting you between sessions
  • To invoice you and keep the business records the law requires
  • To understand which pages and tools help people, using totals from Google Analytics
  • To meet my legal, insurance and professional obligations

I don't sell, rent or trade your information. I won't add you to a mailing list unless you ask to join one, and every marketing email I send will include a way to unsubscribe.

How the burnout check-in handles your answers

  • Your readout. When you finish, your answers go through a small program I run on Cloudflare to Anthropic's Claude AI service, which writes your readout. I don't save your answers on my own systems. Under its standard terms, Anthropic deletes this data within 30 days, unless its systems flag it for a policy check or the law requires it to keep the data longer. Anthropic doesn't use this data to train its AI models.
  • Abuse prevention. To stop people overloading the tool, Cloudflare keeps a count of requests from your IP address for one hour, then deletes it.
  • When you enter your email address. Your readout appears on screen, and I also send it to you through Resend, an email delivery service. A copy of your email address and your readout also comes to me through Formspree, a form service, so I know who has taken the check-in. Your readout summarises your answers across four areas and may quote words you wrote. I won't email you again unless you contact me or agree to hear from me.
  • When you skip the email. Your readout appears on screen only, and I don't receive your email address or a copy of your readout. Save or screenshot it if you want to keep it, because it's gone once you close the page.

The readout is for self-reflection. It isn't a medical or psychological assessment.

When I would share information without your consent

Coaching is confidential. I will only share what you tell me without your consent when the law allows or requires it, which includes:

  • when I believe sharing it is necessary to lessen or prevent a serious threat to your life, health or safety, or someone else's
  • when a court order or law requires it

If this ever applies, I will tell you first whenever it's safe and lawful to do so.

How I protect and keep your information

I protect the accounts that hold your information with strong, unique passwords and two-factor authentication, and I'm the only person with access. I keep client records in a secure, access-controlled system, separate from my general email.

  • Enquiries and check-in readouts from people who don't become clients: deleted from Formspree and my inbox 12 months after I receive them.
  • Client records: kept for 7 years after our last session, as NSW health records law requires, then securely deleted. If you were under 18 when I collected them, I keep them until you turn 25.
  • Google Analytics data: kept for Google's standard retention period, then deleted automatically.

If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, I will tell you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, as the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme requires.

Seeing, correcting or deleting your information

You can ask for a copy of the information I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Email me at the address below. I will reply within 30 days, and it won't cost you anything. If the law requires me to keep something, such as client records within the 7-year period, I will explain what I have to keep and why.

Making a complaint

If you think I've mishandled your information, email me first and tell me what happened. I will acknowledge your complaint within 7 days and give you a full response within 30 days.

If you're not satisfied with my response, you can complain to:

  • The Office of the Australian Information Commissioner: oaic.gov.au or 1300 363 992
  • The NSW Information and Privacy Commission, for complaints about health information: ipc.nsw.gov.au or 1800 472 679

Changes to this policy

I will update this policy when my services or tools change, and I will change the date at the top when I do. If a change affects how I use information you've already given me, I will tell you before it takes effect.
